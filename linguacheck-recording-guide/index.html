LinguaCheck Recording Guide

The shot list and teleprompter used to record the LinguaCheck demo video: what to show and what to say, scene by scene. View the LinguaCheck repository.

Space = start/pause scrolling · ↑↓ = speed · Esc = close

Before you press record

  1. Open the LinguaCheck app on the "Confirmed errors" tab and refresh it first.
  2. Pick a quiet room, put the light in front of you, and the camera at eye level.
  3. Close everything private: email, other tabs, notifications. No API keys or passwords on screen.
  4. Do a 20-second test recording and play it back. Check your voice is loud and clear.
  5. Record each scene separately if that's easier, and join them later.

Target length: about 3 minutes. If you run long, shorten the "Who benefits" scene.
